# Restaurant Card Design Description

## Overview
This card design presents a food item from a restaurant in a visually appealing format, designed for a food delivery or restaurant discovery application.

## Card Components

### 1. Food Item Image
- **Position**: Central/main area of the card
- **Content**: A top-down view of a white, round bowl containing a stir-fried dish
- **Visual Details**:
  - The dish appears to be noodles or rice with various ingredients
  - Ingredients visible include:
    - Green leafy vegetables (spring onions or spinach)
    - Small pieces of paneer or tofu (white/cream colored)
    - Other darker, sauced elements
  - Color palette: Yellowish-brown base with vibrant greens and white/cream accents
  - Background: Dark surface (possibly wooden table) visible around the edges of the bowl
- **Purpose**: Primary visual element to attract users and showcase the food item

### 2. Promotional Overlay
- **Position**: Top-left corner of the card, overlaying the food image
- **Design**:
  - Dark grey rectangular banner
  - White text displaying "FLAT ₹75 OFF"
- **Purpose**: Highlights a discount offer to attract customers

### 3. Rating Badge
- **Position**: Below the main food image, on the left side of the card
- **Design**:
  - **Background**: Green rectangular badge/container with rounded or square corners
  - **Icon**: White star icon (⭐) positioned before the rating number
  - **Rating Display**: White text displaying "4.1" (rating out of 5)
  - **Layout**: Star icon and rating number are horizontally aligned within the green badge
  - **Color Contrast**: Green background with white text/icon for high visibility and readability
  - **Size**: Compact badge size that doesn't overwhelm the card but is clearly visible
- **Visual Appearance**: 
  - The green badge creates a prominent visual element that stands out against the card background
  - The white star icon immediately communicates that this is a rating/quality indicator
  - The "4.1" rating is displayed in a clear, readable font
- **Purpose**: Displays restaurant/food item rating to build trust and credibility, helping users quickly assess quality

### 4. Restaurant Name
- **Position**: Below the rating badge, centered horizontally
- **Design**:
  - Larger, dark grey font
  - Text: "The Wok Kitchen"
- **Purpose**: Identifies the restaurant/brand name

## Design Characteristics
- **Layout**: Vertical card layout with image at top, followed by rating, then restaurant name
- **Color Scheme**: 
  - Dark grey for promotional banner and text
  - Green for rating badge
  - White for text on colored backgrounds
  - Natural food colors in the main image
- **Typography**: Clear, readable fonts with appropriate sizing hierarchy
- **Visual Hierarchy**: Image → Promotional offer → Rating → Restaurant name

## User Experience
The card design effectively combines appetizing food photography with key information (promotion, rating, restaurant name) to help users quickly make ordering decisions. The layout is clean and information is easily scannable.
